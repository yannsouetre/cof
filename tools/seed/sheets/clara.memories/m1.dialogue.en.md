# What she used to say — fragments

*My grandmother's sentences, the summer I was eight. Not in order. I'm not even sure of all of them.*

— Eat first, draw after. Huts don't fly away.

— Listen. (evening, the window open) Can you hear the river? It speaks louder at night.

— A plan is good. But a hut is built with your hands.

— Keep your notebook. One day you won't remember, and it will.
