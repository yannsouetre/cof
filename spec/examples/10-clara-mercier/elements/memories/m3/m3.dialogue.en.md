# What stayed with me — a few lines

*A March evening, Saône embankment. I don't remember all of it; these are the pieces that stayed.*

— Excuse me. The Confluences museum, is it that way?
— … Yes. Right at the end. Are you… are you sure you're all right?
— Quite. You're walking? So am I.

(later, in front of a dark shop window)

— Why are your screens so heavy?
— Heavy?
— They heat. They pull. Where I come from we'd say they are afraid of silence.

(I no longer know exactly where)

— So you draw light things.
— I try.
— Good. Keep trying. That is what one notices, from far away.
