Character.AI → COFan open file for your characters

Save your Character.AI bots

Your character and your conversations in one open file (.cof): your conversations become its memories, and you can then give it looks, a voice, presets. Your conversations stay private: they never leave with a shared file. Everything happens in your browser: nothing is sent anywhere.

1Your files

Drop your files here, or clickA card (PNG or JSON) and your conversations (.jsonl, .json, .zip) — all at once

    A file is not recognised? Report it (without your conversations or personal data)

    Where do I get these files?
    • CAI Tools (browser extension): « Download Character » (PNG or JSON card), « Download Tavern chat » (one conversation) or « Download History » (all conversations of a character, a .zip).
    • PersonaText (personatext.com, Character.AI exporter): one .personatext.json file with the card, the avatar and the open conversation, with its dates.
    • Character.AI's official export (Profile › Settings › Account › Manage Account & Data › Export Data): a .zip e-mailed after 3 to 4 weeks, link valid 7 days. Request it now. Its structure is not documented: it is read as well as possible, and you are told who speaks when it has to be guessed.
    • SillyTavern chats (.jsonl) work too.
    • These tools use your own Character.AI session; this page never asks for a password and never contacts Character.AI.
    • An export that is not recognised? Report it (without your conversations or personal data)

    2Check

    The character

    COF asks for an age for every face (a rule that protects minors): the character's age as the avatar shows it — for a non-human avatar, the age it looks. Otherwise, import without the avatar.

    A character made by someone else stays theirs: your file is for personal use (no redistribution, no AI training).
